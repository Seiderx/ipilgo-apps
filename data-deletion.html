Delete your IpilGo data

Last updated: October 5, 2026

You can ask us to delete your IpilGo account and everything linked to it at any time. This also applies if you signed in with Google or Facebook.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject IpilGo data deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address of your IpilGo account (or the email on your Google or Facebook account if you signed in that way), so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile, travel preferences, bookings, reviews, favorites, itineraries, AI assistant history, and notifications. For destination owners, your owner account is removed and your destination is unlisted.

Signed in with Facebook? You can also remove IpilGo from Facebook: open Facebook, go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Apps and Websites, select IpilGo, and tap Remove. Then email us as described above so we delete the data stored in IpilGo.

What we may keep

Payment records needed for accounting are handled by our payment provider, PayMongo, under their own retention rules.
