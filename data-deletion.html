Delete your IpilGo data

Last updated: November 1, 2026

You can delete your IpilGo account and everything linked to it at any time, straight from the app. No approval is needed. This also applies if you signed in with Google or Facebook.

Delete from the app

  1. Open the IpilGo app (tourist or owner) and go to Profile.
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Yes, I'm sure.
  4. Type your username to unlock Delete account, then tap it. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

When deletion has to wait

So nobody loses money or a planned visit, the app asks you to settle these first:

  • Tourists: an upcoming booking you have already paid for. Cancel it first so any refund reaches you. Unpaid upcoming bookings are cancelled for you, and the owner is told.
  • Owners: any upcoming booking at your spot. Decline it, or wait until it is finished.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile and photo, travel preferences, reviews, favorites, itineraries, AI assistant history, check-ins and notifications. For destination owners, your owner account is removed and your listing is taken off the app.

What we keep

Past bookings and their payment records stay in the owner's and the Tourism Office's records for accounting, with your name replaced by "Deleted user" and your phone number removed. Card and e-wallet payments are also kept by our payment provider, PayMongo, under their own retention rules.

Can't open the app? Email salvadorprincekie@gmail.com with the subject IpilGo data deletion request from your account's email address, and we will delete your data within 30 days.
Signed in with Facebook? You can also remove IpilGo from Facebook: open Facebook, go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Apps and Websites, select IpilGo, and tap Remove.
