IpilGo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the IpilGo Tourist app, the IpilGo Owner app, and the IpilGo admin website, a BSCS thesis project for Ipil, Zamboanga Sibugay.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and optional phone number.
  • Google or Facebook sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook", we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that service. We do not receive your password or post anything on your behalf.
  • Travel preferences: interests, budget range, and travel style you choose in the app.
  • Activity: bookings, reviews, favorites, itineraries, and AI assistant conversations.
  • Location: only while you use route and map features, to show directions. We do not track you in the background.
  • Destination owners: destination details and photos you upload.

2. How we use it

To run your account, personalize destination recommendations and itineraries, process bookings, send booking updates, and improve the app's AI features.

3. Sharing

Booking details are shared with the destination owner so they can serve you. Some features use outside providers that receive only what that feature needs: Supabase (data storage and sign-in), Google Maps (maps and routes), OpenWeatherMap (weather), Google Gemini (AI recommendations and chat), and PayMongo (GCash and Maya payments; IpilGo never sees your full payment details). We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored with Supabase and protected by access rules so each account can only reach its own data. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

5. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you may access, correct, or ask us to delete your personal data. You can edit most details in the app's Profile screen. To delete your account and data, see Data Deletion.

6. Children

IpilGo is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information without parental consent.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
