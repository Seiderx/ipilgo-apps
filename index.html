How to install

IpilGo is installed straight from this website instead of the Play Store, so Android asks two extra questions. Both are normal.

Android phones only. IpilGo is currently available on Android devices only. iPhone support is not yet available.
  1. Tap Download APKOr scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  2. Open the downloaded fileFind it in your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installingAndroid asks:
    Allow from this source? Tap Allow
  4. Install anywayPlay Protect may say:
    Unrecognized app. Tap Install anyway

Questions

Play Protect warned me. Is IpilGo safe?

Yes. Android shows that warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store, not because something is wrong with IpilGo. Only download it from this page. See the Privacy Policy for what the app collects.

Which phones can install it?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer. To check, open Settings, then About phone, and look for "Android version".

Does it need internet?

Yes. Maps, routes, weather, the AI assistant, bookings and GCash or Maya payments all need mobile data or Wi-Fi.

How do I get updates?

When a new version is out, the app shows an "Update available" message. Tap Update now, then Install. Your account and data stay as they are.

It says "App not installed".

You probably have an early test version on your phone. Uninstall IpilGo once, then install it again from this page.
